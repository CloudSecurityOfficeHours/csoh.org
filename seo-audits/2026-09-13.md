## Warnings (6)

All six are the same persistent meta-length edge cases carried from the 2026-08-31 audit; none worsened.

1. **Meta description 4 chars over 160** - `about-shawn-nunley.html` (164 chars). "Shawn Nunley - founder of Cloud Security Office Hours and Solutions Architect at Wiz. 42 years in tech, the last 30 in security, now specializing in cloud security." Consider trimming "42 years in tech, the last 30 in security, now" → "now 42 years in tech" to reclaim ~4 chars.

2. **Meta description 1 char over 160** - `security-policy.html` (161 chars). "Responsible disclosure policy for CSOH - how to report security vulnerabilities, what's in scope, our response timeline, and recognition for coordinated reports." Shorten "coordinated reports" → "coordinated disclosure".

3. **Meta description 1 char over 160** - `privacy.html` (161 chars). "Cloud Security Office Hours (CSOH) privacy policy: no cookies, no cross-site or marketing trackers, just cookieless page-view analytics. We never sell your data." Trim "or marketing trackers" → "trackers" saves 17 chars.

4. **Title 6 chars under 50** - `cspm-vs-cnapp.html` (44 chars). "CSPM vs CNAPP vs CWPP vs CIEM vs DSPM - CSOH". Acronym density limits options; consider appending "Explained" or expanding "CSOH" to "Cloud Security".

5. **Title 5 chars under 50** - `cloud-security-certifications.html` (45 chars). "Cloud Security Certifications Compared - CSOH". Append "2026" or swap "CSOH" for "Cloud Security Office Hours" to reach 50+.

6. **Title 1 char under 50** - `news.html` (49 chars). "Cloud Security News - Cloud Security Office Hours". Borderline; adding "Daily" or "Latest" reaches target.

## Opportunities (3)

7. **resources.html page weight** (880 KB raw HTML, up from 850 KB on 2026-08-31 as 18 new resources were added). All 535 images carry lazy loading; the weight is structural. Monitor as the file approaches 1 MB; pagination or virtualization becomes more compelling at that threshold.

8. **breach-timeline.html heading density** - 46 H2s on one page. Each kill-chain entry maps to an H2, which is semantically correct, but search engines may weight individual H2s less when there are this many. Moving per-breach H2s to H3 under thematic H2 groupings (e.g., by threat actor or technique cluster) is a low-risk opportunity.

9. **sitemap `lastmod` accuracy** - sitemap.xml has grown to 280 URLs (all with lastmod set). Confirm `site-update-deploy.yml` reliably refreshes `lastmod` on each syndicated-content deploy so dates don't drift stale on infrequently-touched pages.
