## Warnings (6)

All six are minor meta-length edge cases when measured against the strict 50-60 char title / 140-160 char description targets; they all pass the automated tool's wider 30-65 / 100-165 thresholds.

1. **Meta description slightly over 160 chars** - `about-shawn-nunley.html` (164 chars). "Shawn Nunley - founder of Cloud Security Office Hours and Solutions Architect at Wiz. 42 years in tech, the last 30 in security, now specializing in cloud security." Could trim "42 years in tech, the last 30 in security, now" to reclaim ~5 chars.

2. **Meta description 1 char over 160** - `security-policy.html` (161 chars). "Responsible disclosure policy for CSOH - how to report security vulnerabilities, what's in scope, our response timeline, and recognition for coordinated reports." Borderline - shorten "coordinated reports" → "coordinated disclosure" or trim elsewhere.

3. **Meta description 1 char over 160** - `privacy.html` (161 chars). "Cloud Security Office Hours (CSOH) privacy policy: no cookies, no cross-site or marketing trackers, just cookieless page-view analytics. We never sell your data." Trim "or marketing trackers" → "trackers" saves 17 chars.

4. **Title 6 chars under 50** - `cspm-vs-cnapp.html` (44 chars). "CSPM vs CNAPP vs CWPP vs CIEM vs DSPM - CSOH". The acronym density limits options; consider appending "Explained" or swapping "CSOH" for "Cloud Security".

5. **Title 5 chars under 50** - `cloud-security-certifications.html` (45 chars). "Cloud Security Certifications Compared - CSOH". Append "2026" or swap "CSOH" for "Cloud Security Office Hours" to reach 50+.

6. **Title 1 char under 50** - `news.html` (49 chars). "Cloud Security News - Cloud Security Office Hours". Borderline; adding any word (e.g., "Daily" or "Latest") reaches target.

## Opportunities (3)

7. **resources.html page weight** (850 KB raw HTML). Largest file by far; second place is chat-resources.html at 588 KB (correctly noindexed). No structural SEO issue at current lazy-load coverage (517/517 images lazy), but worth monitoring as content grows.

8. **breach-timeline.html heading density** - 46 H2s on one page. Each kill-chain entry maps to an H2, which is semantically correct, but search engines may weight individual H2s less when there are this many. Could consider moving per-breach H2s to H3 under thematic H2 groupings (e.g., by threat actor or technique cluster). Low-risk opportunity only.

9. **sitemap `lastmod` accuracy** - resources.html shows `2026-08-27`, which matches recent automated updates. Confirm `site-update-deploy.yml` reliably refreshes `lastmod` on each syndicated-content deploy so the date doesn't drift stale on infrequently-touched pages.
