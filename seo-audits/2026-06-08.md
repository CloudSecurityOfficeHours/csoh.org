## Warnings (1)

1. **privacy.html meta description is 226 characters** (target 140-160)
   - Current: `"The Cloud Security Office Hours (CSOH) Privacy Policy. We use no cookies and no cross-site or marketing trackers (just privacy-friendly, cookieless page-view analytics), and never sell or share your data. Plain-English policy."`
   - Google typically renders 155-160 characters then truncates; the second sentence is unlikely to be shown in SERPs.
   - Suggested fix: trim to ≤160 chars, e.g. `"No cookies, no cross-site trackers, no data sales. CSOH's plain-English privacy policy - cookieless analytics only."`
