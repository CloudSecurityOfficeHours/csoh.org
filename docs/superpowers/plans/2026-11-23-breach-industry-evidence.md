# Breach industry tags: evidence

Each tag on a kill chain card rests on the sentence quoted here from that chain's breach page.
This record covers the chains as of 2026-11-23; a chain added later carries its evidence in the body of the commit that adds it.
Every quote was checked by script as text that appears on the page. A tag means the page says an
organization in that industry was compromised, lost data, money or access, or took services offline
because of the attack (see the design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-breach-industry-search-design.md`).

## Leads from outside the pages

Victims the readers recalled from their own knowledge, which the breach pages do not mention.
They are unverified and are not tags; each would need a sourced sentence on the page first.
The leads that held up were sourced and added to the pages; see
[2026-11-23-breach-victim-sources.md](2026-11-23-breach-victim-sources.md).

- **LiteLLM / TeamPCP:** The cited PyPI incident report covers 'LiteLLM and Telnyx'; the Telnyx Python package was reportedly also trojanized by TeamPCP. Telnyx would be Telecom, but the page body never says Telnyx was hit. Trivy is maintained by Aqua Security (Technology); the page names Trivy as compromised but not Aqua by name.
- **GTG-1002:** As far as I know Anthropic did not publicly name any of the victim organizations.
- **Oracle E-Business Suite / Cl0p (CVE-2025-61882):** Publicly reported Cl0p EBS victims (leads only) include Harvard University (Education), Envoy Air, an American Airlines subsidiary (Transportation), The Washington Post (Media), Logitech (Manufacturing), GlobalLogic (Technology), Allianz UK (Finance), Cox Enterprises, Canon, Mazda, Schneider Electric and Emerson (Manufacturing/Energy), Dartmouth College and the University of Phoenix (Education).
- **Shai-Hulud:** CrowdStrike-published npm packages were reportedly trojanized in the September 2025 wave (Technology). Shai-Hulud 2.0 (November 2025, mentioned on the page without victims) reportedly hit packages from Zapier, PostHog, Postman and ENS Domains; ENS would be a Crypto lead for that wave.
- **npm debug / chalk:** Public tracking put actual stolen funds at a very small amount (on the order of hundreds of dollars to about $1,000). The same npmjs.help phishing campaign reportedly also compromised DuckDB's npm packages (Technology).
- **Salesloft Drift / UNC6395:** Victims that publicly confirmed Salesforce data access include Cloudflare (a cited source on the page, but not described there as hit), Zscaler, Palo Alto Networks, Proofpoint, Tenable, Qualys and PagerDuty, all Technology.
- **SharePoint ToolShell (CVE-2025-53770):** Publicly reported ToolShell victims (leads only) include the US National Nuclear Security Administration / Department of Energy, the Department of Education, NIH, the Rhode Island General Assembly and the Florida Department of Revenue (Government; NNSA also Energy), plus European government bodies and universities.
- **UNC6040:** Publicly reported victims of the 2025 Salesforce vishing wave include Cisco, Qantas, Allianz Life, Chanel, Pandora, Adidas, LVMH brands (Louis Vuitton, Dior, Tiffany), Workday, Air France-KLM and TransUnion; none is named on the page (leads for Transportation and Finance). Okta and Microsoft 365 appear on the page only as platforms whose victim accounts were reused against, not as compromised companies.
- **tj-actions/changed-files:** No downstream victim organization is named; Endor Labs' 218 leaking repositories are not identified by owner or industry on the page.
- **UNC5537 / Snowflake:** Other publicly reported UNC5537 victims not named on the page include Advance Auto Parts and Neiman Marcus (Retail), LendingTree/QuoteWizard (Finance), Pure Storage (Technology) and Los Angeles Unified School District (Education).
- **Polyfill.io:** Contemporary reporting named sites loading cdn.polyfill.io at the time, reportedly including JSTOR, Intuit and the World Economic Forum, and Censys listed domains tied to large brands; none appears on the page.
- **Midnight Blizzard / Microsoft:** CISA Emergency Directive 24-02 (April 2024) said the actor exfiltrated email correspondence between US federal agencies and Microsoft: a Government lead not on the page. HPE separately disclosed Midnight Blizzard access to its cloud email around the same period; not on the page.
- **Storm-0558:** The other ~25 organisations are not named or described by industry on the page; public reporting at the time named few others (e.g. a US congressman's account was reportedly notified).
- **MOVEit Transfer / Cl0p:** Named victims in public reporting (not on the page) include payroll provider Zellis and its clients British Airways, BBC and Boots; Shell; the US Department of Energy; Louisiana OMV and Oregon DMV; Maximus; Genworth and CalPERS (via PBI); Johns Hopkins; and several large accounting firms. These would add Transportation, Media, Retail, Energy and Technology leads.
- **3CX / X_TRADER:** Symantec and others reported downstream X_TRADER victims including two critical infrastructure organizations in the energy sector and two financial trading organizations; 3CX's own downstream victims were reported to skew toward crypto firms. Leads for Energy, Finance and Crypto; none is on the page.
- **LastPass:** Researchers (e.g. Krebs, Taylor Monahan, ZachXBT) later linked tens of millions of dollars in cryptocurrency thefts to cracked LastPass vaults; the page does not mention this, so it is a Crypto lead only.
- **0ktapus / Twilio:** Group-IB's 0ktapus report and later disclosures named other victims, e.g. DoorDash, Mailchimp and Klaviyo; Group-IB said the targets were concentrated in technology, telecom, crypto and finance firms. None of these are on the page. Okta disclosed in August 2022 that the actor, via Twilio's console, saw phone numbers and OTP messages of a small number of Okta customers.
- **Okta / LAPSUS$:** The two accessed customer tenants have not been publicly named, so no customer industry can be added. LAPSUS$ breached Microsoft, Nvidia, Samsung, Ubisoft and T-Mobile in the same period, but those are separate incidents, not this chain.
- **Log4Shell:** Belgium's Ministry of Defence confirmed a Log4j-related attack in December 2021 (Government). CISA advisory AA22-320A: Iranian state actors compromised a US federal civilian agency through Log4Shell in an unpatched VMware Horizon server (Government). Vietnamese crypto platform ONUS was breached through Log4Shell in its Cyclos software and extorted (Crypto). Minecraft: Java Edition servers were among the first widely exploited targets (Media/gaming).
- **Kaseya VSA / REvil:** About a dozen New Zealand schools and kindergartens were disrupted through an affected MSP (Education). In Sweden, SJ (state rail) ticketing and the Apotek Hjärtat pharmacy chain reported disruption (Transportation, Healthcare). Kaseya took its own SaaS VSA servers offline as a precaution.
- **Codecov Bash Uploader:** Mercari (Japanese e-commerce marketplace) disclosed that source code and some customer and merchant data were exposed through Codecov (possible Retail lead). monday.com also disclosed impact.
- **SolarWinds:** Microsoft disclosed that the actor viewed some of its source code; Mimecast had a certificate compromised; Malwarebytes reported email access (all Technology). Other confirmed government victims included DHS, Commerce (NTIA), State, Energy (including NNSA) and NIH.
- **Capital One:** The DOJ indictment said Thompson also stole data from more than 30 other organizations and used hijacked compute for cryptomining; press reports named some, such as Vodafone, Ford, Michigan State University and the Ohio Department of Transportation (verify before use).
- **event-stream:** Copay is BitPay's wallet; BitPay's own app shared the affected Copay codebase (versions 5.0.2 to 5.1.0). The page does not say whether funds were actually stolen.
- **Kevin Mitnick / Novell:** The federal case covered intrusions and source-code theft at Motorola, Nokia, Fujitsu, Sun Microsystems and NEC (telecom equipment, electronics and computer makers). They would be leads for Telecom/Manufacturing/Technology if the page stated they were compromised.

## Judgement calls

Tags kept, each a reading of the page that could go the other way:

- **ARTEX / Exposed Secrets:** Government, Energy, Legal and Education all rest on one Todyl sentence saying the actor "reached" 27 organizations, including MSP clients in those sectors. "Reached" is read as access. No victim is named. This is the only Legal tag. Nonprofits are named too, but there is no Nonprofit industry and one sentence is not enough to add one.
- **PHP2Shell / PAN-OS:** Government rests on "a US government-affiliated network", which could be an agency or a contractor.
- **GMO Onamae.com Desktop Cloud:** Finance rests on five FX firms reporting affected customers, whose accounts saw unauthorized trades. The broker platforms themselves were not compromised.
- **UNC6040:** "fashion houses" are tagged Retail, as consumer brands that sell direct.
- **npm debug / chalk:** Crypto rests on the page saying the payload rewrote wallet transactions and sent funds to the attacker. No victim or loss figure is given.
- **UNC5537 / Snowflake:** Ticketmaster is tagged Media (live entertainment); Retail would also be defensible.
- **ChaosDB** and **Microsoft AI Research SAS Token:** tagged Technology although both were found by researchers, because the pages say the data was reached or exposed.
- **event-stream:** Technology although the maintainer handed over publish rights when asked, because the project was then used to ship malware.

Tags left off:

- **PHP2Shell / PAN-OS:** European government sites, Dutch hospitals, the Port of Rotterdam and a Sophos portal went offline as a precaution, not because they were attacked.
- **GTG-1002:** the page lists the sectors targeted (technology, finance, chemical manufacturing, government) and says a small number of intrusions succeeded, without saying which.
- **3CX / X_TRADER:** Trading Technologies makes trading software; the page presents it as a software vendor, so it is Technology, not Finance.
- **tj-actions/changed-files:** Coinbase was targeted but the attack could not use its secrets, so no Crypto tag.
- **Storm-0940 / ConnectWise:** manufacturing and telecom compromises are attributed to an overlapping group, not shown to be the same actor.
