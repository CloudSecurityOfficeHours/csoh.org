# Breach victims: sources for new industry tags

Victims that the breach pages did not name, sourced so that their industries can be tagged.
Every source below was fetched and the claim checked against its text. Each page gets one
sentence at the end of its summary, the sources in its Primary Sources box, and named victims
in its JSON-LD `mentions`.

## Judgement calls

- **LastPass:** the Crypto tag rests on researchers' links and on a March 2025 forfeiture complaint that does not name LastPass. The sentence attributes the link ("investigators believe") and gives LastPass's statement that law enforcement had not made it aware of conclusive evidence.
- **Capital One:** the added victims are allegations in the superseding indictment, which does not name them.
- **UNC6040:** the victims confirmed breaches but did not name Salesforce; the tie to this campaign is BleepingComputer's reporting, and Air France-KLM declined to confirm it.
- **UNC5537 / Snowflake:** the Los Angeles Unified School District says the data was held by a vendor and its own systems were not breached; the sentence says so.
- **Kaseya VSA / REvil:** the SJ and Apotek Hjärtat disruption is SVT's reporting; neither company made a statement.
- **Shai-Hulud:** Trust Wallet says the leaked secrets "likely" led to the drain; the sentence keeps the hedge.

## Dropped leads

- **Oracle EBS:** Logitech (its release names no platform; the tie rests on Cl0p's leak site), Cox Enterprises (confirmed, but a conglomerate with no clear industry), Canon (no data loss confirmed), Mazda (targeted, no leakage found), Schneider Electric and Emerson (leak-site listing only).
- **SharePoint ToolShell:** the US Department of Education, the Rhode Island General Assembly and the Florida Department of Revenue (unnamed sourcing only); NIH and Croatia's Ruđer Bošković Institute were confirmed but add no industry beyond Government.
- **UNC6040:** Louis Vuitton (South Korea's regulator found malware, not a vishing call); TransUnion (one of two sources was the extortion group).
- **3CX / X_TRADER:** crypto companies (Kaspersky shows targeting, not confirmed victims).
- **Shai-Hulud:** ENS Labs (its own post says packages "may be affected").
- **Capital One:** Vodafone, Ford, Michigan State University and the Ohio Department of Transportation (none confirmed; none named in any court or DOJ document).
- **Midnight Blizzard:** Hewlett Packard Enterprise (a separate intrusion).
