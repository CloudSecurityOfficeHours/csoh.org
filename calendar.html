Subscribe to the calendar

Google Calendar

Use Add to Google Calendar. Google asks you to sign in, then adds "Cloud Security Office Hours" under Other calendars. Changes the organisers make show up on their own.

Apple Calendar, Outlook and everything else

Subscribe to the iCal feed. If your app does not open webcal links, add a calendar by URL and paste the feed address. Subscribing, rather than importing, is what keeps a moved or cancelled session in step.

Just the Friday series

If you only want the recurring Friday 7am PT slot as a one-time import, the add-to-calendar file on the sessions page still does that.

How this page stays current

A scheduled GitHub Actions job reads the calendar's public iCalendar feed, expands the weekly series along with any moved or cancelled week, and rewrites the cards above as plain HTML. Nothing here runs in your browser and nothing is embedded from Google. The job's own file, update-calendar.yml, is commented line by line like the rest of the site's workflows, and the renderer is tools/sync_calendar.py.
